# Video production guide

Format: 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps, H.264 MP4, clear mono or stereo narration. Use presenter plus slides, with a live notebook capture on slide 29. Keep notebook text readable at 1080p. Record deliberate pauses on slides 27–29. Verify audio, captions, slide order, duration, rights, synthetic-data disclosure and final SHA-256 before release.
