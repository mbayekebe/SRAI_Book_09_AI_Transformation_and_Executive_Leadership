# From AI Ambition to Institutional Decision

Many AI strategies begin too late: with a list of tools or pilots. Institutional transformation begins earlier—with mandate, beneficiary value, evidence and authority. Experience in census modernization, information-system integration, results-based monitoring and field modelling consistently shows that definitions, ownership, validation and capability determine whether technical work survives operational reality.

A credible AI portfolio therefore combines weighted opportunity scoring with separate mandatory gates. It tests sensitivity, records uncertainty and names the people authorized to scale, redesign, defer or stop each initiative. This is the central lesson of SRAI Book 9 Lesson 1: strategy is visible not only in what leadership funds, but also in what it refuses to scale without evidence.
