AI strategy is not a catalogue of tools. It is a disciplined way to decide what an institution should fund, defer, redesign or stop.

In Book 9, Lesson 1 of SRAI, I connect responsible AI strategy with lessons drawn from years of consulting in agricultural statistics, information systems, results-based monitoring, capacity development and field modelling.

The lesson includes an executable portfolio notebook, readiness assessment, sensitivity analysis and decision records. The cases are experience-derived; the notebook data are synthetic.
